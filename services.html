A CLEAR PATH FROM IDEA TO LAUNCH

How we work together.

01 / BRIEF

Understand the goal.

Share your audience, desired outcome, references, timeline and budget range.

02 / SCOPE

Agree on the plan.

Confirm deliverables, price, schedule, revision rounds and responsibilities in a proposal.

03 / BUILD

Create and review.

Review the work against the agreed brief and test the key experiences.

04 / HANDOFF

Make it yours.

Receive agreed files, setup notes and an editing walkthrough. Ongoing support is scoped separately.

How long does a project take?

Turnaround depends on scope, content readiness and integration access. The schedule is confirmed after reviewing your brief.

What should I prepare?

Your goals, existing brand assets, copy, references and a list of required pages or workflow steps. Account connections are set up through the provider’s authorized flow.

Are the showcased workflows running here?

No. They demonstrate the supplied workflow structure. Running a workflow requires configuration, authorized service connections and testing.
